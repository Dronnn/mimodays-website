Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026. The short version: your moments stay on your device and never reach me. There is no account and no MimoDays server.

Who makes MimoDays

MimoDays is made by Andrew Maier, an independent developer. Questions about this policy: mimodays@mrmaier.com.

What stays on your device

Your photos, videos, voice notes and their transcripts, notes, places, collections and movie projects are kept in the app’s storage on your iPhone or iPad. MimoDays never sends them to me or to anyone else. The one thing the app sends on its own is a location, to name a place (see Permissions).

If iCloud Backup is on for your device, Apple may include this data in your device backup, under Apple’s privacy policy.

Permissions

  • Camera and microphone: only while the camera is open or you record a voice note. The microphone is used for video only when its sound is on.
  • Location: optional, and only while you use the app. To name a place, its coordinates go to Apple’s map service, which handles them under Apple’s privacy policy: when a new moment is ready to save, when you look for a nearby place or search for one, and when you import photos with their places. You choose how precise places found for you are, and you can turn them off. A place you pick yourself is kept as you picked it, and an imported photo keeps the location stored in its file.
  • Photos: MimoDays can only add to your library, to save what you export. Photos you import are chosen in Apple’s picker, and MimoDays sees only the ones you pick.
  • Notifications: surprises are scheduled on your device; no server sends them.
  • Face ID: App Lock asks iOS to check it; MimoDays never sees your face data.
  • Speech: on iOS 26 and later, voice notes are transcribed on your device, in the languages it supports. iOS may first download the speech model from Apple.

Backups and exports

A backup is a file you create and save where you choose, for example in Files or iCloud Drive. Whatever you export or share goes only where you send it, and is then handled by that service.

Tips

Optional tips are in-app purchases handled entirely by Apple. I receive no payment or contact details.

No tracking

MimoDays has no ads, no analytics and no third-party trackers. If you choose to share crash reports with app developers in iOS Settings, Apple may pass anonymous crash data to me.

This website

This site is static: no cookies, no analytics, no forms. Its hosting provider may process technical data such as your IP address to deliver the pages and protect the site.

Your choices

You can delete any moment. It waits in Recently deleted for 30 days, unless you delete it there for good. Settings › Privacy & App Lock › Delete all data removes everything at once. Deleting the app removes all its data from the device. Backups and exports you made stay where you saved them.

Children

MimoDays collects no personal data from anyone, children included. The app isn’t directed at children under 13.

Your rights

The app gives me no personal data about you, so there is nothing of yours for me to access, correct, export or delete. Everything is on your device and under your control. If you write to me, I use your email only to reply and delete it when the conversation is over.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
