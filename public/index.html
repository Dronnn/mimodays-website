Privacy

A private story,
not another social feed.

MimoDays is made for your memories, not for followers. No public timeline, no likes, no pressure to share. You decide what to keep, export or delete.

No account

Open the app and start. There is nothing to sign up for.

Stays on your iPhone

Your moments live on your device, not on a MimoDays server.

Place only if you want it

Location is optional, and you choose how precise it is.

Your backups, your files

Save a backup to Files or iCloud Drive whenever you like.

No ads, no tracking

No analytics and no third-party trackers, in the app or on this site.

Export everything

Take your photos and videos out, as you see them or as the originals.

Questions

Do I need to capture something every day?

No. A surprise is an invitation, not an assignment. Skip it and nothing is lost: there are no streaks and no notifications about what you missed.

Which iPhones does it work on?

Any iPhone with iOS 18 or later, and any iPad with iPadOS 18 or later.

Can I use only one camera?

Yes. A moment can keep just one view: its selfie window can be removed. On iPhones that can’t film with both cameras at once, MimoDays uses one.

Where are my memories stored?

On your iPhone, in MimoDays. Nothing goes to a MimoDays server. To keep a copy, make a backup in Settings › Backup and save it to Files, iCloud Drive or a computer.

Can I get my original photos and videos out?

Yes. Export a moment as you see it in MimoDays, with the selfie window, or as separate photos and videos, one per camera. You can also export many moments at once as one archive.

Does it cost anything?

No. Every feature is free. If MimoDays helps you, you can leave an optional tip; it doesn’t unlock anything.
