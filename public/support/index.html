Support

Something not working, or an idea? Write to mimodays@mrmaier.com. I read every message and reply as soon as I can.

Before you write

Many answers are in the app: Settings › Help & about.

Moving to a new iPhone

On the old iPhone, open Settings › Backup and make a backup. Save it to iCloud Drive, another place in Files, or a computer. On the new iPhone, install MimoDays, open Settings › Backup, tap Restore from a backup and choose that file.

Surprises don’t arrive

Check that notifications for MimoDays are on in iOS Settings, and that surprises aren’t paused in MimoDays › Settings › Surprises. A Focus can hold them back too.

Camera or microphone not working

Allow the camera and microphone for MimoDays in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security.

Helpful details

When you write, add your iPhone model, the iOS version and the MimoDays version (Settings › Help & about).
