Terms of Use

Last updated: November 18, 2026. Plain terms for a simple app.

The licence

MimoDays is licensed to you under Apple’s Standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement, together with these terms.

Free, with optional tips

Every feature is free. Tips are voluntary, unlock nothing, and are handled by Apple under its purchase and refund rules.

Your moments are yours

Everything you capture belongs to you. MimoDays keeps it on your device and claims no rights to it.

Keep your own backups

MimoDays keeps no copy of your moments. They exist on your device, in backups you make, in what you export and in iCloud Backup if it’s on for your device. Please back up regularly: if the device is lost or the app is deleted without a backup, your moments can’t be recovered.

Be kind with the camera

Respect the people around you and the law where you are when you capture a moment, especially when filming others.

MimoDays is provided as is. To the extent the law allows, I’m not liable for lost data or indirect damages.
